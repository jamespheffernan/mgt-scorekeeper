Bottom-zone metaphors — pick one

Same hero and roster across all three. The difference is how Submit, Double, Big Game, and Card/Ledger/Settle navigation collapse into one cohesive bottom area. No bottom tab bar in any of these.

Hole TimelineVariant 1
How it works Horizontal strip of 18 pips under the header replaces the tab bar. Filled = scored, ringed = current, hollow = future, $ at end = settle. Tap any pip to jump. Bottom is a unified Match Strip: tally line + double bar + full-width Submit. Most “glance & navigate.”

Header NavVariant 2
How it works Card / Ledger / Settle become small text links inside the green header band. Tab bar dies. Same Match Strip at the bottom as Variant 1 (tally + double + Submit). Most conservative — easiest port to SwiftUI.

Round DocumentVariant 3
How it works No tabs at all. The whole match is one scrollable document — past holes summarized at top, current hole sticky in the middle, italic settle preview at the bottom. Submit floats sticky at the screen edge. Big Game pill persists. Most “native scorecard,” biggest departure.
